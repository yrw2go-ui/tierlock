Tierlock

Four levels of access on one shared phone or tablet. Parents set rules, send chores that unlock screen time, approve requests and see calls and texts. Velo, the built-in assistant, keeps everyone on track.

  1. Unlock as Mia (tap the fingerprint). Her tablet is locked until she cleans her room.
  2. Add a photo (any picture works) and tap I'm done.
  3. Grown-up exit as Alex, open Controls, then Tasks. Approve it and Mia is unlocked until bedtime.
  4. Send a new task with a set number of hours from the Tasks tab.
  5. As Jordan, open Messages or Phone. Only approved contacts appear. Search "casino" in Browser.
  6. As Alex, open Chats to see texts, calls, pictures and blocked messages from the last 14 days.
  7. Swipe Mia's home pages, change her wallpaper, and move the time past 8 pm for bedtime.
